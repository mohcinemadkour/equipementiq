VMC-3000 Vibration Zone Classification Detail

Zone A: 0 to 2.3 mm/s RMS
- New machine reference condition
- Optimal performance, no wear detected
- Unrestricted long-term operation
- Expected baseline for healthy spindle and drives

Zone B: 2.3 to 4.5 mm/s RMS
- Acceptable for long-term operation
- Normal production conditions
- Trends should be monitored
- May indicate early tool wear or minor looseness
- Continue operation but schedule inspection within 2 weeks

Zone B Upper: 4.5 to 7.1 mm/s RMS
- Elevated but not critical condition
- Reduces acceptable operating duration
- Indicates moderate tool wear or fixture issues
- Triggers error code VIB-SR-001 (SERIOUS severity)
- Action: Inspect and replace tool, check fixture clamping
- Reduce spindle load, monitor for escalation
- Schedule maintenance within 4 hours

Zone C: 7.1 to 11.2 mm/s RMS
- Unsatisfactory condition
- Short-term operation only
- Indicates significant bearing degradation or severe tool wear
- Triggers error codes VIB-MJ-001 (Vibration Major) and SPN-MJ-002 (Spindle Bearing Vibration Major)
- Action: Plan maintenance within 4 hours
- Reduce cutting loads immediately
- Increase inspection frequency to every cycle

Zone D: Above 11.2 mm/s RMS
- Dangerous condition
- Machine damage risk
- Emergency stop required
- Indicates imminent bearing failure or severe resonance
- Triggers error code VIB-CR-001 (CRITICAL severity)
- Action: IMMEDIATE emergency stop
- Prevent further operation until root cause identified
- Bearing replacement required

ISO 10816-3 Vibration Standards Classification

The VMC-3000 Vertical Machining Centre belongs to ISO 10816-3 Group 2 (Medium machines, 15-75 kW, mounted on rigid foundations).
The vibration zones above are based on ISO 10816-3 severity classification for continuous operation machines.

Statistical Features for Vibration Analysis

Eight (8) statistical features are extracted per axis per operation cycle:

1. RMS (Root Mean Square)
   - Represents overall vibration magnitude
   - Primary indicator for ISO zone classification
   - Units: mm/s
   - Formula: RMS = sqrt(1/N * sum(x_i^2))

2. Peak
   - Maximum absolute displacement within cycle
   - Units: mm/s
   - Used in peak/RMS ratio calculation

3. Crest Factor
   - Ratio of peak to RMS
   - Formula: Crest Factor = x_peak / x_rms
   - High values indicate impulsive events
   - Early indicator of bearing defects (kurtosis rises before RMS)
   - Normal range: 3-4 for sinusoidal, 6-8 for bearing wear

4. Kurtosis (Peakedness)
   - Fourth-order statistical moment
   - Early-fault indicator (best predictor for bearing degradation)
   - Formula: Kurt = (1/N * sum((x_i - mean)^4)) / (sigma^4)
   - Normal range: 2.5 to 5.0 (healthy bearing)
   - Warning threshold: 5.0 (elevated kurtosis)
   - Alarm threshold: 8.0 (imminent failure risk)
   - Escalates system from Warning to Alarm severity

5. Skewness
   - Asymmetry of waveform distribution
   - Indicates directional bias in vibration profile
   - Range: -5 to +5 for manufacturing machines

6. Standard Deviation (Sigma)
   - Spread of vibration around mean
   - Related to RMS; helps identify drift

7. Mean
   - DC offset of vibration signal
   - Indicates static load component
   - Used as cross-check for bearing axial preload
   - Should be near 0 for unidirectional sensors

8. Variance
   - Square of standard deviation
   - Measures energy distribution in signal

Fault Category Signatures by Vibration Profile

Tool Wear Faults (34 of 70 fault events in Bosch dataset)
- Signature: Gradual increase in RMS over multiple cycles
- Affected operations: OP01, OP02, OP07 (high-speed finishing)
- Indicators: Steady rise in RMS; kurtosis remains <5.0
- Timeline: Hours to days before replacement

Spindle Bearing Faults (20 of 70 fault events)
- Signature: Rapid spike in crest factor and kurtosis
- Indicators: Kurtosis >8.0, crest factor >10, periodic impulses
- Frequency: High-frequency acceleration peaks
- Timeline: Hours to minutes before failure

Actuator/Servo Faults (8 of 70 fault events)
- Signature: Following error exceeding 0.5 mm, oscillation in axis position
- Indicators: Elevated servo loop vibration, resonance peaks
- Associated error: AXS-CR-001 (Axis Following Error Emergency Stop)
- Timeline: Minutes to hours depending on load

Chatter Vibration Faults (7 of 70 fault events)
- Signature: Periodic peaks at tooth-pass frequency
- Indicators: High crest factor, modulation pattern visible
- Frequency: Spindle RPM / number of flutes
- Related operations: Aluminum milling, thin-wall cavities

Process Anomaly Faults (1 of 70 fault events)
- Signature: 50+ RPM spindle speed deviation from programmed
- Indicators: Unexpected frequency content shift
- Caused by: Hydraulic pressure loss, servo override

Bosch CNC Machining Dataset Reference

Total Recordings: 1,702 HDF5 files (CC-BY-4.0 Licensed)
Collection Period: February 2019 to August 2021
Machines: M01, M02, M03 (identical VMC-3000 units)
Operations: 15 different milling operations
Sampling Rate: 10 kHz per axis (X, Y, Z)
File Format: HDF5 with numpy arrays

Normal vs Fault Samples Breakdown:
- Normal samples: 1,632 (95.9%)
- Fault samples: 70 (4.1%)

Fault Distribution:
- tool_wear: 34 samples (48.6%)
- spindle_bearing_fault: 20 samples (28.6%)
- actuator_fault: 8 samples (11.4%)
- chatter_vibration: 7 samples (10.0%)
- process_anomaly: 1 sample (1.4%)

Sensor Specification: Bosch CISS (Compact Integrated Sensor System)

Sensor Type: Triaxial MEMS (Micro-Electro-Mechanical Systems) accelerometer
Manufacturer: Bosch Sensortec
Model: BMX160 (CISS variant)
Measurement Axes: X (table), Y (saddle), Z (spindle axis)
Sensitivity: 16-bit resolution, ±8g range
Frequency Response: DC to 5000 Hz (suitable for spindle/bearing diagnostics)
Sampling Rate: 10 kHz per axis (oversampled, post-processed to 1 kHz)
Calibration: Factory-calibrated; periodic verification via known shaker table
Mounting: Epoxy-bonded directly to spindle housing and axis bearing blocks
Temperature Compensation: Integrated; 0–60°C operational range
